## Conventions

- **Windows.** Grey is `BASELINE_90` (2025-09-01 to 2025-11-29); blue is `FINAL_90` (2025-11-30 to 2026-02-27). Differences between them are descriptive: the eligible populations are not identical, so they are never within-profile change.
- **Deviation charts.** A zero line means "behaves like peers with similar activity". Solid lines with filled circles benchmark against similar watch hours; dashed lines with open squares against similar active days. The two controls stay separate rather than being averaged into a score the analysis never defined.
- **Censored shares** are hatched grey, because an unobserved outcome is unresolved rather than a third outcome.
- **Counts.** Where a figure prints `n`, it counts the profiles that met the evidence threshold behind the plotted rate, not every profile in the group.
